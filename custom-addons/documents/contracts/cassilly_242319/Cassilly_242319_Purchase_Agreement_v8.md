## SECTION 9 — CLOSING COSTS

**9.1 Seller Responsibilities:**
- Title insurance premium (owner's policy)
- Deed preparation
- Estate-related title curative work
- Recording fees for deed
- Prorated real estate taxes through date of closing
- Seller concession of 3% ($7,050.00) toward Buyer closing costs

**9.2 Buyer Responsibilities:**
- Lender fees and loan origination
- Appraisal fee
- Inspection fees
- Mortgage title insurance (lender's policy)
- Recording fees for mortgage
- Prepaid taxes, insurance, and escrow reserves

**9.3 Closing Cost Explanation:**
The Seller concession of 3% ($7,050.00) is requested due to the following: the original purchase agreement contemplated owner/seller financing at 2% interest, which would have resulted in substantially lower total financing costs for Buyer over the life of the loan. When that structure was rejected by legal counsel, Buyer was required to obtain conventional bank financing at prevailing market rates (estimated 6-7%), significantly increasing total interest expense. The concession partially offsets this increased cost to Buyer and is standard practice in residential real estate transactions.
