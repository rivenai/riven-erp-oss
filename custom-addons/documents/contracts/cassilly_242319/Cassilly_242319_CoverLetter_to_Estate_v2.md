### Changes Made Per Your Request:

**1. "Subject to Court Approval" Language (replacing "Estate Authorization")**
The contract now reads "subject to court approval" in Section 2.2, Section 7.5, and the new Section 17 (Court Approval Clause). This language replaces all prior references to "estate authorization" throughout the document. Section 17 includes a 10-business-day filing deadline and a 90-day termination provision if court approval is not obtained.

**2. Closing Cost Explanation**
Section 9.3 provides a detailed explanation of the Seller concession request. In summary: the original deal was structured with owner financing at 2% interest, which would have cost Buyer approximately $66,126 in total interest. When that arrangement was rejected by legal counsel and Buyer moved to conventional bank financing, the estimated total interest rose to approximately $231,676 over 30 years. The 3% concession ($7,050) partially offsets this additional cost to Buyer. This is standard practice in residential transactions where Buyers come in at or near full asking price.

**3. Closing/Settlement Statement**
Section 9 now includes a full breakdown of Seller and Buyer closing cost responsibilities. A formal settlement statement (HUD-1 or Closing Disclosure equivalent) will be prepared by the closing attorney, Borders & Borders, PLC, prior to the closing date.
